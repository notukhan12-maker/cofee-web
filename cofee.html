Our Philosophy

Crafted with Obsession. Served with Elegance.

Founded in 2018, Velvet Brew emerged from a singular uncompromising vision: to elevate coffee from a commodity to high art. We partner directly with legendary micro-lot farmers across high-altitude estates in Ethiopia, Colombia, and Sumatra.

Every bean is roasted on our custom vintage cast-iron roaster, tuned daily by master artisans to unlock complex flavor notes ranging from jasmine florals to dark cocoa and wild berries.
